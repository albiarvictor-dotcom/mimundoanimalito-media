Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

"Mi Mundo Animalito" is a personal, non-commercial content-production project. This page covers the small backend automation tool used internally to publish short animated video clips to social platforms, including TikTok, via each platform's official developer APIs.

Who uses this tool

This tool is used by a single individual (the owner) to publish content to social accounts they own and control. It is not a public product, has no end-user sign-up, and does not knowingly interact with or collect data from any third party.

What data the tool handles

The tool stores and processes:

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by TikTok (and other connected platforms) to the owner's own account, used only to publish the owner's own video content via that platform's API.
  • The video files, titles and captions produced for the owner's own YouTube channel, which are the content being published.
  • Basic operational logs (e.g. which video was published, when, and whether the API call succeeded) kept for the owner's own troubleshooting.

No data about TikTok end users, viewers, or any third party is collected, stored, or processed. No data is sold or shared with any other party.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are kept only as long as needed to keep the integration working and are revoked/deleted if the integration is discontinued. Operational logs are kept for the owner's own record-keeping.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner via the contact details on the associated YouTube channel, "Mi Mundo Animalito".

Mi Mundo Animalito — internal automation tool, not a public product or service.
